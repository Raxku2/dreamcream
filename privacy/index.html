# **Client Anonymity Protocol** **The Doctrine of Absolute Discretion.** At the absolute pinnacle of society, anonymity is not merely a preference; it is the rarest and most fiercely guarded commodity. You dictate the shape of the world, and in return, DreamCream protects your identity, your palate, and your whereabouts with uncompromising severity. This protocol outlines our solemn vow of silence regarding your engagement with our institution. --- ### **I. The Curation of the Dossier** To orchestrate a bespoke gastronomic masterpiece worthy of your legacy, we must acquire select intelligence. We do not gather ‘data’; we curate a highly classified private dossier. This may include: * **Estate and Aviation Coordinates:** Precise delivery routing for your primary residences, superyachts, and private aircraft tail numbers. * **The Palate Profile:** Intimate psychological and sensory observations gathered by our Master Gastronomers to engineer your exclusive flavour profile. * **Dynastic Directives:** Dietary mandates, allergen protocols, and specific preferences for members of your family and esteemed guests. * **Private Financial Liaisons:** Encrypted routing handled exclusively through highly vetted, elite financial institutions. ### **II. The Execution of Excellence** Your intelligence is utilised for a singular, unapologetic purpose: to ensure flawless execution of your commissions. Whether you require a daily indulgence in London or an emergency gala delivery mid-flight over the Atlantic, your dossier allows our global concierge to anticipate your exact desires without requiring you to repeat them. ### **III. The Vault of Secrecy** Your bespoke recipe and personal coordinates are not stored; they are vaulted. * **Cryptographic Isolation:** All digital dossiers are protected by military-grade cryptographic architecture, rendering your information impenetrable to the outside world. * **The Vow of Silence:** Every Master Gastronomer, logistics artisan, and concierge at DreamCream operates under a draconian Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). To speak of a client’s commission to the uninitiated is an unforgivable breach of our institutional code. ### **IV. The Circle of Trust** DreamCream exists entirely outside the pedestrian world of data brokering. Your dossier is never sold, traded, or leveraged. We share vital delivery coordinates only with our most trusted, legally bound aviation and maritime couriers, ensuring your commission arrives with silent precision. No external entity is ever granted access to your flavour profile or personal legacy. ### **V. Absolute Sovereignty** You command empires, and you command your presence within our institution. You hold absolute sovereignty over your dossier. Should you wish to modify your coordinates, update your palate profile, or demand the total erasure of your records from our vaults, a single directive from you or your Chief of Staff is all that is required. We serve at your pleasure. Your secrets are ours to keep. --- **By submitting a commission inquiry, you acknowledge that you are entering into a confidential pact of the highest order with DreamCream.** For direct inquiries regarding the safeguarding of your estate's intelligence, your representatives may contact our Chief Privacy Officer directly via your private concierge liaison. ---
